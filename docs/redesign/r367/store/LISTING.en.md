## App Store (iOS)

**Name (30 chars):**
Ulara — University Operating System

**Subtitle (30 chars):**
From lecture to mastery

**Promotional text (170 chars):**
An independent Arabic-first learning platform built in Libya: interactive
lectures, online exams, a research library, training tracks, and a mastery
matrix built from your real performance.

**Description:**

Ulara is the university's operating system: one platform for learning,
exams, the library, training, and institutional governance — Arabic-first,
with every operation documented.

For students: interactive lectures that pause at checkpoints and grade
instantly; online exams with a question bank and automatic marking; a
research library with recorded borrow and return; training tracks with
certificates; a mastery matrix read straight from your results and
revision; an academic assistant that works inside the platform today
(tailored explanations and revision plans from your actual performance).

For teachers: the lecture and exam author, automatic objective marking,
attendance and engagement tracking, and one class dashboard.

For administration, quality, and ownership: a full registrar archive,
read-permission quality oversight, councils and programs, live indicators,
and an audit trail for every action — permissions are measured by role,
not by trust.

Technical features: a hardened session (survives network loss; revoked
only by decision), offline reading for open content, in-platform
notifications, quiet optional haptics and UI sounds, full Arabic (RTL)
support with Arabic-Indic or Latin numerals, and light/dark themes on the
same paper-and-ink ground.
