# Critique: RIGHT vs LEFT (Reference)

## 1. Illustration Scale & Quality
- LEFT's architectural illustration spans ~55% of viewport width (~800px of 1440px) and sits at the compositional center. RIGHT's isometric pixel-art sketch occupies only ~28% width (~410px) and ~33% height (~320px of 900px) — roughly **half the visual mass** of the reference.
- Quality gap: LEFT is a high-fidelity, hand-drawn architectural elevation with depth, hatching, shadows, and texture. RIGHT is low-resolution pixel art with visible aliasing and crude detail — reads as placeholder, not hero art.
- LEFT's illustration is grayscale/ink-toned and harmonizes with the paper background. RIGHT introduces heavy blue annotation strokes (dimension lines, "H 29.0", "56.0") that fight the palette (see §5).

## 2. Typography
- LEFT's headline is tight, grotesk, near-black, high-tracking control, left-aligned, occupying 2 lines at ~64–72px. RIGHT's Arabic headline is a rounded humanist sans with loose letterforms and generous line-height (~1.5), feeling soft and generic vs LEFT's engineered precision.
- RIGHT's headline is ~72px but justified right with ragged visual density; LEFT balances headline weight against the artwork — RIGHT stacks headline + illustration vertically, diluting both.
- Subcopy: LEFT's is ~16px, right-blocked with clear hierarchy and a "DISCOVER MORE" link. RIGHT's subcopy is ~13px, right-aligned, almost invisible against the frame; the CTA button ("ابدأ محاسباتك") is small (~28px tall) vs LEFT's strong typographic link system.
- RIGHT mixes four type treatments (Arabic sans, pixel/LCD numerals, Latin mono in "56.0", logo) without LEFT's mono/sans discipline.

## 3. Hairline Frame Coverage
- LEFT: complete hairline grid — outer frame, vertical section dividers in the nav, top/bottom rules, crop marks (+) at corners, full horizontal measure strips. Frame coverage ≈ 100% of edges, symmetric.
- RIGHT: frame is broken — left edge has scattered, disconnected annotation labels ("مصفحة - سقف أروقة", "01/09 الأونام") with an arbitrary vertical line at ~40% height; a full-width blue rule at ~48% height slices the hero randomly. No crop marks, no corner registration, no vertical dividers in the top nav (nav items float without separating hairlines). Frame coverage ≈ 60%, asymmetric.
- LEFT's dashed measure strip runs full width; RIGHT's dashed line stops mid-frame.

## 4. Composition & Dead Space
- LEFT: single focal stack — illustration centered, headline anchored bottom-left, subcopy bottom-right, all four corners resolved. Dead space is evenly distributed (~15% margins all around).
- RIGHT: dead space is uneven — large empty bands at top-left (~35% of left half unused), bottom-right corner empty, and the illustration floats off-center-right. Left ~35% of the canvas is essentially unused.
- RIGHT adds noise instead of structure: floating compass, two crosshairs, squiggles, scattered scroll indicator — decorative, not compositional, unlike LEFT's two deliberate orange accent squares anchored to the artwork.
- Headline occupies the full top width pushing illustration into the lower half, creating a cramped 45/55 split instead of LEFT's airy layered layout.

## 5. Signal-Color Discipline
- LEFT: exactly **one** signal color (orange-red #FF4C00-ish), used in ~5 micro-doses: two 12px squares, "CONTACT US", "DISCOVER MORE", ACCEPT button. Total accent coverage < 1% of pixels.
- RIGHT: two competing signal colors — blue (dimension lines, logo accent, "ابدأ من هنا", progress "0%", target marker) and orange-red (a single ~16px marker in the illustration). Blue alone accounts for ~3–4% of pixels and appears in 7+ locations, several of them structural (a full-width blue rule). This violates LEFT's single-accent, sub-1% discipline.

---

## Top 3 Gaps
1. **Illustration fidelity & scale**: pixel-art placeholder at ~half the reference's width, with no tonal harmony — the single biggest quality gap.
2. **Hairline frame system**: broken, asymmetric frame with a stray full-width blue rule and orphaned annotations vs LEFT's complete, symmetric grid registration.
3. **Signal-color discipline**: dual accent palette (blue + orange) at ~4× the reference's accent coverage, diluting the crisp one-accent system.
